How much is the HYROX365 programming?

The HYROX365 Affiliation and with it the HYROX365 program is $130 per month. This includes full access to the HYROX Performance Hub, the all-in-one HYROX Training platform with programming, mini-events, educational resources, and marketing materials. ‍

Important Note: You can only use the HYROX365 program and offer it in your gym if you are affiliated with HYROX as an official HYROX Training Club. To affiliate click here.
