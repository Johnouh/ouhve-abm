Why Choose
HWPO Affiliate?

Get access to three HWPO Affiliate programs designed specifically for group-based training. Each program is designed to help your coaches with time management, technical cues and focuses, scaling options, warm-ups, and more, with seasonal add-ons for specific offers and focuses.

CLASS
Designed to get your members fit through all ten general physical skills, with a heavy emphasis on strength, skill, and metcons. Class is the core program of HWPO Affiliate and includes programming for 7 days a week.

SWEAT
Get your members warmed up and worked out quickly and effectively with around 45 minutes of timed intervals. Each workout is high-intensity, requires minimal equipment, and focuses on low-skill conditioning. Sweat includes programming for 6 days per week.

FOCUS
Dive into the details with 8 week speciality programs designed to improve specific aspects of your member’s fitness and explore new activities as a community. The Focus program includes programming for 2-4 days per week.

Got Questions? We’ve got answers.
I am already a HWPO Affiliate customer. Can I still use Train?

Yes! With the HWPO Affiliate x Train integration, HWPO Affiliate Programming and session planning will automatically be uploaded to your Train account each week and you'll have the power of Train workout tracking at your fingertips!

How many days per week do you program?

HWPO Class: 7 days / week
HWPO Sweat: 6 days / week
HWPO Focus: 2-3x / week

Do I need to be a PushPress customer to use Train?

Yes. We've focused a lot of effort into building a really strong integration between PushPress Core and Train. But if you are an Online Trainer, book a demo and we'll help you figure out if Train is a good fit!

Ok I’m in! How do I get HWPO Affiliate Programming into my Train account?

Just click the link above to log into your Train account, and you can start accessing HWPO in minutes!

What is included in the HWPO Affiliate Membership?

The HWPO Affiliate membership includes HWPO Class, HWPO Sweat, HWPO Focus programming and movement clinics. In addition to the programming, you’ll have access to our members-only online community platform called CIRCLE.

In CIRCLE we can schedule live calls, post important information such as training-block information and upcoming events, and you can also ask questions and receive feedback.

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fee

Is there a contract?

No. Nada. We're not fans of contracts. We only create products we know our gyms will see value from.

Do you have Training Cycles?

We do have training cycles. These cycles are designed to help members increase their athletic ability in the areas of strength, power, endurance, gymnastics, etc. The start and end point of our training cycles throughout the year is the CrossFit Open.

How long are your training cycles?

8-10 weeks

Do you include a Class Plan?

We do not include a class plan but we have time stamps in the documentation that allow coaches to manage their time effectively. We also provide coaching cues that give coaches specific technical points to focus on in the day.

Do you include Extra Work?

Included in the affiliate program is an Extra / Accessory which is not to be included in the 60 minutes of class but is designed to complement the training that was done during class.

What if I need help with my HWPO Affiliate account?

If you’re having trouble with your account, please reach out to our team by sending an email to contact@hwpotraining.com.
