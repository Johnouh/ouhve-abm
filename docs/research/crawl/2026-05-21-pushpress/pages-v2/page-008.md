Scheduling that works the way you coach

Set up 1-on-1 appointments, semi-private time slots, or small group sessions with capacity caps — all from a single calendar. Whether you run a 4-person semi-private block or a packed 12-person group, PushPress handles the logistics so you can focus on coaching.

Billing and payments on autopilot

Automate recurring billing for personal training packages, session packs, and small group memberships. Accept credit cards, manage failed payments automatically, and give clients a clear view of their account — no awkward money conversations.

Deliver programming that keeps clients coming back

Create and deliver tailored workouts for individual clients or entire small groups. Push programming directly to your clients’ phones, track their progress over time, and build programs that scale from 1-on-1 to semi-private without recreating everything from scratch.
