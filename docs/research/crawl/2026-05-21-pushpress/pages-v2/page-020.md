Got Questions? We’ve got answers.
What tracks are included?

Affiliates will have access to the following tracks:

Performance Track (6 days per week) - CrossFit style group coaching track. Don’t worry, we’ve customized this into two versions: Full Effort (RX) and Full Victory (Customized Version)
Fitness Track (6 days per week) - A functional fitness track that features dumbbells and resistance training. This track mimics the Performance track so the two tracks can be interchangeable.
Functional Strength Track (5 days per week) - Hypertrophy training – aka higher reps, less weight. This track is for anyone who wants to get toned and strength train.
Cardio Track (5 days per week) - Endurance sessions that cover a variety of aerobic modalities.
Running Track (3 days per week) - Run specific sessions to help members improve anything from their 1-mile time trial to running their best 10k races.
What else is included when I sign up?

Monthly newsletters, week-in-review videos from your coaches, exclusive deals with Invictus partners, daily coaching notes, a Facebook group to connect with fellow affiliates, and the opportunity to join us for further education of your coaching staff through our Invictus University. Affiliates will also receive a Welcome Packet that includes a standard customization document so that you and your coaches will be equipped with easy-to-make customizations for the daily workouts.
