Why Choose The Daily Fix Programming?
Three Pillars: Belly. Body. Brain.

The Daily Fix is the world’s only free tool for gym owners to deliver metabolic health. Every day delivers an easy to follow recipe, a classic workout, and a short educational video or article. Together, these create lasting habits that improve physical, mental, and metabolic health.

Engage Your Members Beyond the Gym

The Daily Fix distills complex metabolic research into easy-to-follow actions that anyone can follow. Complete customization, add our recipe and article section to your current gym programming or use it as a standalone nutrition program.

Ready for Your Members - Right Now

The Daily Fix translates complex metabolic science into practical daily habits that actually work for busy members. It integrates directly into your member experience to boost engagement, results, and retention.
