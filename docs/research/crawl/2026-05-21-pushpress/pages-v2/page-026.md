Got Questions? We’ve got answers.
I am already a PRVN customer. Can I still use Train?

Yes! With the PRVN x Train integration, PRVN programming and session planning will automatically be uploaded to your Train account each week and you'll have the power of Train workout tracking at your fingertips!

Do I need to be a PushPress customer to use Train?

Yes. We've focused a lot of effort into building a really strong integration between PushPress Core and Train. But if you are an Online Trainer, book a demo and we'll help you figure out if Train is a good fit!

How much is PRVN?

PRVN offers unlimited access for $150/mo.

I already subscribe to PRVN through another platform. Can I switch to Train?

Yep! Mention the switch to our Train onboarding team, and we can help you migrate member data from your old system!

Ok I’m in! How do I get PRVN into my Train account?

Just click the link above to log into your Train account, and you can start accessing the PRVN in minutes!

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fee

Is there a contract?

No. Nada. We're not fans of contracts. We only create products we know our gyms will see value from.

How does Train sync with my PushPress system?

Yes, when you are ready you can turn on the member sync and sync members between platforms.
