Questions? We've Got Answers.
What are the top marketing automation tools for gyms?

PushPress Grow is purpose-built marketing automation for independent gyms: lead capture from web/social/walk-in, automated email and SMS sequences, a visible sales pipeline, a website builder, and GymHappy review generation, all integrated with PushPress Core member data. Other options include ABC Glofox (best for boutique studios already on the platform), Mindbody (best for multi-location operators), and Wodify Rise (best for existing Wodify CrossFit affiliates).

What is gym marketing automation?

Gym marketing automation is software that handles lead follow-up, trial-to-member conversion, reactivation, and class promotion without manual work from the gym owner. It captures leads from website forms, Facebook, Instagram, and walk-ins; fires automated email and SMS sequences; tracks each prospect through a sales pipeline; and reports on which lead sources are converting.

How much does PushPress Grow cost?

PushPress Grow is $329/month as a standalone add-on to PushPress Core, or bundled into the Full Stack plan with Core Pro, Train, and the Branded Member App.

Does PushPress Grow integrate with PushPress Core?

Yes — Grow is built on the same data layer as PushPress Core, so leads, trial members, and active members move automatically between systems. There's no manual sync, CSV export, or third-party integration needed. Gyms running Core plus Grow generate an average 48% more leads per month than Core-only gyms.

What software helps with getting gym leads?

PushPress Grow captures gym leads from website forms, Facebook lead ads, Instagram, and walk-ins, then routes them into automated email and SMS follow-up sequences within seconds. The connected sales pipeline shows where every lead is in the conversion process, so nothing falls through the cracks.
