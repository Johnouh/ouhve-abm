Got Questions? We’ve got answers.
I am already a Mayhem customer. Can I still use Train?

Yes! With the Mayhem x Train integration, Mayhem Athlete Affiliate programming and session planning will automatically be uploaded to your Train account each week and you'll have the power of Train workout tracking at your fingertips!

Do I need to be a PushPress customer to use Train?

Yes. We've focused a lot of effort into building a really strong integration between PushPress Core and Train. But if you are an Online Trainer, book a demo and we'll help you figure out if Train is a good fit!

How much is Mayhem Gym programming?

Mayhem offers unlimited access for a flat $129/mo and comes with a 14-day Free Trial. This includes access to all Mayhem Affiliate Programs: Mayhem Affiliate, Burgener Strength, Faith, CrossFit Kids, Pre-Teens, Teens, L.I.F.E. Class, Endurance, Running, Spin, At-Home, and BodyBuilding

What other benefits do I get for signing up with Mayhem?

Our all-inclusive programming provides daily coaching videos, programming notes, warm ups, cool downs, scaling options, affiliate support, coach development resources.

How much programming does Mayhem give to their affiliates?

The main Affiliate programming includes 60 minutes classes, 6 days a week, Monday through Saturday, with an optional cardio/aerobic piece each Sunday. A sample week of our can be found here.


Is there a setup fee?

No setup fee

When do I receive the programming?

The programming for the following week is released by Friday (at the latest) of each week. We also send out a pdf of the main Affiliate programming to share with your coaches.

How does Train sync with my PushPress system?

Yes, when you are ready you can turn on the member sync and sync members between platforms
