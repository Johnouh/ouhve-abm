FAQ
Got questions? We’ve got answers.
What is PushPress Train?

PushPress Train is workout tracking and programming for any gym that wants to track workout performance. CrossFit boxes, strength and conditioning gyms, semi-private and personal training studios all use it. Coaches program daily workouts, members log results in the PushPress member app, and progress shows up in lifetime PRs, benchmark history, and attendance streaks. Train is the only major workout tracker that runs natively inside the same platform handling your billing, scheduling, and member management.

Do I need to be a PushPress customer to use Train?

Generally, yes. We've focused a lot of effort into building a really strong integration between PushPress Core and Train. But if you are an Online Trainer, book a demo and we'll help you figure out if Train is a good fit!

How much does PushPress Train cost?

Pricing starts at $79/mo and varies based on the number of members in the system.

Is PushPress Train a Wodify, SugarWOD, or BTWB alternative?

Yes. Train replaces Wodify, SugarWOD, BTWB, and TrainHeroic for gyms that want workout tracking integrated with the rest of their operations. The difference is the integration. Workouts, results, attendance, billing, and retention data all live in one platform. Migrating workout history from Wodify or SugarWOD is included.

Can members track personal records and workout history with PushPress Train?

Yes. Members log results in the PushPress member app and see lifetime PRs, benchmark history (Fran, Murph, max lifts, custom benchmarks), and attendance streaks. PRs are flagged automatically, so coaches can celebrate them publicly. That kind of recognition is one of the most reliable drivers of member retention.

Does PushPress Train work for CrossFit affiliates and partner programming?

Yes. Train pulls programming directly from CrossFit Affiliate Programming (CAP), Mayhem, HWPO, PRVN, Invictus, and Bolder Affiliate. Affiliate owners can push the daily WOD without copying and pasting. Train also handles non-CrossFit programming for strength gyms, hybrid gyms, martial arts academies, and personal training studios.

How does Train sync with my PushPress system?

Train is built to share data with Core. Workout completion shows up next to membership status, attendance, and retention metrics. That integration is what separates Train from standalone workout-tracking apps.
