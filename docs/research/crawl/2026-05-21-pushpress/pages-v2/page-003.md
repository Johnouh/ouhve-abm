Questions? We've got answers.
What is PushPress Core?

PushPress Core is the gym management platform that runs your business. Built by gym owners for gym owners, it handles memberships, billing, scheduling, check-ins, payments, digital waivers, and reporting in one place. Core is purpose-built for CrossFit boxes, martial arts academies, strength gyms, and small group training studios. It is not generic appointment software adapted for fitness.

How much does PushPress Core cost?

Core comes in three plans, all month-to-month with no contracts.

Core Free: $0/month. 4.99% + $0.30 per credit card transaction. 2.89% + $0.30 ACH.
Core Pro: $159/month. 2.89% + $0.30 credit card. 0.79% + $0.30 ACH.
Core Max: $229/month. 2.75% + $0.30 credit card. 0.79% + $0.30 ACH.

For most gyms processing more than $20K/month, the lower processing rates on Pro or Max recover the subscription cost several times over.

Is PushPress Core a good fit for small or new gyms?

Yes. Core Free at $0/month is a real platform, not a trial. New gyms can run their entire business on it from day one. Memberships, billing, scheduling, member app, all included. Migration from Mindbody, Zen Planner, Wodify, GymMaster, Pike13, or Glofox is included at no cost. Most gyms upgrade to Pro when revenue hits around $10K/month and the lower processing rates start saving more than the $159 subscription.

Does PushPress Core automate billing and recurring memberships?

Yes. Core runs recurring billing, prorated charges, family memberships, drop-ins, retail, and packages. Failed payments retry automatically through dunning. Members can update their own card or bank account. ACH is available on Pro and Max at 0.79% + $0.30, which lowers processing costs significantly for long-term members.

Does PushPress Core handle check-ins and digital waivers?

Yes. New members sign waivers digitally during sign-up. No paper, no separate DocuSign integration. Check-ins happen through the member app, a front-desk tablet, or auto-detection at class start. Coaches see attendance in real time, and the data flows directly into retention reporting.

Can I switch from Mindbody, Wodify, or Zen Planner to PushPress Core?

Yes, and migration is free. The PushPress onboarding team imports members, payment plans, billing schedules, and class history from Mindbody, Wodify, Zen Planner, GymMaster, Pike13, Glofox, and most other gym platforms. Most gyms are fully live within two to three weeks with no disruption to members.
