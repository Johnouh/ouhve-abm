How AI Is Changing the Fitness Industry (What Gym Owners Need to Do Next)

TL;DR: AI in the fitness industry is already reshaping retention, revenue, and operations. This article covers the biggest changes AI is bringing to fitness and what owners can do about it.

Fitness industry trends move fast. AI is already reshaping member behavior, member expectations, and how top gyms operate. Nearly 1 in 2 active consumers have already used an AI-driven fitness solution. 

The operational side of fitness is shifting, too. AI is moving from a standalone feature to infrastructure embedded throughout the systems gyms already use. Operators who have activated it are recovering failed payments, flagging at-risk members 30 days before they cancel, and running re-engagement workflows with no manual input.

This article is for gym owners who want to know what is actually changing and what to do next. We won’t attempt to define AI. Instead, we’ll show you which AI trends in the fitness industry matter for operators and the action you should take.

What’s Actually Changing in the Fitness Industry

AI has immediate, practical implications for the fitness industry. Here is what’s already changing, and what operators should know.

Members now expect personalization

AI in fitness apps, for one, has raised the bar for what members expect from fitness experiences. According to the ABC Fitness’ Summer 2025 Wellness Watch report, nearly 1 in 2 consumers have already used an AI-driven fitness solution. It’s likely that current and prospective members have already tried AI tools for personalized workout recommendations and coaching.

Wearables are accelerating this. Heart rate monitors, recovery trackers, and sleep data now feed directly into AI systems that can adjust recommendations in real time. The line between a member’s fitness app and their coaching experience is blurring.

As Bill Davis, CEO of ABC Fitness, notes: “Members want more than simple tracking. They want their app to reflect real-time guidance tailored to their lifestyle.” 

The action for operators: make sure your communications and offers are personalized to match what members are experiencing everywhere else. Leveraging members’ behavioral data is key.

📝 Read More: 6 Fitness Tech Categories Reshaping Gym Operations

Retention has become a data problem

Retention used to be a relationship problem. Stay close to members, build community, and follow up when someone goes quiet. Good instincts, but limited by what any team could track manually across a full membership base.

AI in the fitness industry has changed the scale of what’s possible. Behavioral signals like drops in attendance and reduced app activity can now be read simultaneously across every member.

The data shows a clear pattern: the shift is from reactive retention to predictive retention. From finding out a member left, to knowing they were about to leave.

📝 Read More: Churn Prediction for Gyms: How AI Spots At-Risk Members


Operations can now run themselves

Failed payment follow-ups, waitlist notifications, booking confirmations, and re-engagement messages all used to require manual coordination. 

Now, AI can handle the trigger-and-respond loop automatically. A failed payment fires a smart retry. A last-minute cancellation triggers a notification to the next person on the waitlist. Admin that consumed hours per week now runs in the background.

There’s a broader case here, too. Members are using AI tools for nutrition tracking, recovery monitoring, and digital coaching outside the gym. Operators who can integrate these metrics into member profiles will have a more complete picture of member health and engagement.

The action for operators: identify the highest-pain manual task your team repeats every week and automate it first.

📝 Read More: 7 Operational Tasks You Should Automate in your Fitness Studio (and How Much Time You’ll Save)

Revenue optimization is moving from manual to intelligent

Traditional upselling relies on staff members to notice an opportunity and act on it. For example, a trainer could spot a member ready for PT. It works, but it largely depends entirely on who’s on shift.

AI is changing the equation. It can identify which members are most likely to upgrade and when, then trigger the offer automatically. 

Behavior-triggered upsells are increasingly replacing blanket promotions. The offer reaches the member at peak motivation, not when the calendar says it’s time to run a promotion.

As Bill Davis describes: “AI can help with more seamless billing and amenity recommendations tailored to individual preferences, offering more targeted upsell opportunities.” This is the direction fitness industry trends are moving, and AI platforms are enabling it today.

AI isn’t a separate product you have to buy

Most gym owners think of AI as something you add on top of your existing setup. The reality is that AI capabilities are already built into modern gym management platforms. 

At ABC Glofox, our AI Churn Predictor, Intelligent Billing, and CRM are features inside the platform your team already uses. The question is whether they’re being used to their full capabilities.

AI isn’t replacing your staff or your culture

The most common concern about AI in the fitness industry is that it makes gyms feel less human. If the machine is flagging risks and sending all the messages, what does that mean for the team?

AI in fitness is about giving your team back the time and energy to do their jobs better. The system handles the operational layer: billing retries, scheduling alerts, triggered outreach. Your team handles the actions that drive loyalty.

“AI will never replace the human connection at the heart of fitness. What it can do is remove the operational complexity that pulls operators away from their members.” – Bill Davis, CEO, ABC Fitness

AI identifies who needs a conversation. Your staff are the ones who have it.

What Gym Owners Should Do Next

Understanding AI trends in the fitness industry provides useful context. Knowing what to actually do is the logical next step. Here are three concrete actions you can take in relation to AI, today.

1 – Audit what’s already available in your platform

Before evaluating new tools, find out what’s already available in the platform you’re using. Most gym owners are sitting on AI capabilities they’ve never activated. At ABC Glofox, our AI Churn Predictor, Intelligent Billing, and automated CRM workflows are all live today.

Ask yourself: does your platform flag at-risk members automatically? Does it optimize billing retries? Does it trigger member outreach based on behavior? If the answer is no, start there.

2 – Pick one use case and switch it on

The temptation when exploring AI in fitness is to plan a comprehensive rollout. Instead, pick the single highest-pain task your team handles manually every week.

For most gyms, that means failed payment follow-ups, at-risk member outreach, or new member welcome sequences. Automate one and put it to work. 

Once one workflow is running on its own, you can start to make the business case for expanding (how much time staff saves on average, how many members AI has helped recover, etc).

“We utilize the [AI-powered At Risk report] weekly to follow up with high churn members. We love it!” – Trey Moser, Body Blueprint

3 – Use your data to set the baseline

AI is only as useful as the data feeding it. Make sure you know your current churn rate, ARPU, and upsell conversion rate. These numbers will tell you where AI will have the biggest impact on your business.

High churn? Start with the AI Churn Predictor. Low ARPU? Start with behavior-triggered upsell workflows. Leaky billing? Begin with Intelligent Billing.

Knowing your baseline gives you something to measure against. When the numbers move, you’ll know exactly why.
