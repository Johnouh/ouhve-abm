Run Clear Schedules and Fill Every Slot

Create a schedule tailored to how your boxing studio operates. Set bag counts, coach capacity, and class formats with ease. Smart waitlists and auto move up features help you keep every session full, protect revenue, and give members a consistent routine.

Set Straightforward Memberships and Pricing

Offer recurring memberships, class packs, and intro trials, all with rules that are simple to manage. Track visits, expirations, and add-ons without using multiple systems or manual work.

Automate Payments and Protect the Calendar

Autopay, deposits, renewal nudges, and reminders help reduce no-shows and missed payments. Add late fees and holds so expectations stay clear and your team spends less time chasing down billing issues.

Smooth and Reliable Member Check In

Use the access method that fits your studio, including a streamlined check in kiosk or door access integrations. Confirm member requirements before class begins and keep entry moving during peak times.

See Attendance and Revenue in One Dashboard

Quickly view class demand, member movement, and revenue. Make decisions about scheduling, staffing, or pricing without spreadsheets or manual exports.

Launch With Confidence

We help configure membership rules, bag-slotting, waitlists, and reminders. Migration is guided end-to-end so you can switch from DIY tools or legacy systems without slowing down the studio.
